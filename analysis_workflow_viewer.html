Automated droplet/capsule image analysis

flowchart TD
    A[Input JPEG images] --> B[Crop fixed region of interest]
    B --> C[Convert image to grayscale]
    C --> D[Estimate background with Gaussian blur]
    D --> E[Subtract image from background]
    E --> F[Normalize and enhance contrast]
    F --> G[Apply fixed intensity threshold]
    G --> H[Remove small objects]
    H --> I[Fill holes and clear image borders]
    I --> J[Label connected particles]
    J --> K[Measure size shape and intensity]
    K --> L{Particle accepted}
    L -->|No| M[Reject object]
    L -->|Yes| N[Convert pixels to physical units]
    N --> O[Add experiment material and nozzle metadata]
    O --> P[Calculate dimensionless droplet diameter]
    P --> Q[Export particle CSV files]
    Q --> R[Export overlays and quality control panels]
