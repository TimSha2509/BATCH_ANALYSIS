# Image-analysis workflow

JPEG images of dark droplets or capsules on a bright background were cropped to a fixed region of interest and converted to grayscale. Spatially varying illumination was corrected by subtracting the grayscale image from a Gaussian-blurred background estimate (sigma = 80 px); contrast values above 5 were enhanced 2.5-fold, and particles were segmented using a fixed intensity threshold of 2. The binary mask was cleaned by removing small objects, filling holes, and excluding border-touching regions, after which connected components with an area of at least 20 px² and a circularity of 0.50–1.10 were retained. Particle area, equivalent circular diameter, perimeter, axis lengths, centroid, shape descriptors, and intensity statistics were determined and converted to physical units using a calibration of 0.01742 mm px⁻¹; the resulting records were combined with experimental, material, nozzle, pump-speed, and oscillation metadata and exported together with labeled overlays and quality-control panels.

The editable presentation diagram is provided in `analysis_workflow.svg`. Its text, boxes, colors, and connectors are native SVG elements and can be edited in PowerPoint (after conversion to shapes), Inkscape, Illustrator, Affinity Designer, or diagrams.net.
